```text
GLP-1 Formulary Why is my GLP-1 drug changing tiers? We regularly evaluate the usage and costs of prescription medications. We’ve found that the rapid increase in the use of weight loss GLP-1s is the main factor increasing costs. To maintain our prescription drug program, plan premiums and access to weight loss medications, tier adjustments are necessary. Are there weight loss GLP-1 drugs that are not covered? Yes, there are weight loss GLP-1 drugs that are not covered. For more information, visit the FEHB Prescription Drug Coverage or PSHB Prescription Drug Coverage page. Can I obtain a weight loss GLP-1 that is excluded or not covered? Yes, you can still obtain a weight loss GLP-1 that is not covered or excluded. In order to do so, your healthcare provider must submit a formulary exception request on your behalf. If your case aligns with the formulary exception guidelines, approval for your weight loss GLP-1 will be granted. Please note that a formulary exception does not change your out-of-pocket costs. You or your provider can download the FEP Traditional Formulary Exception Form (for Traditional FEHB & PSHB) or FEP MPDP Formulary Exception Form (for MPDP FEHB & PSHB) to submit a request. Your provider can also call 1-877-727-3784 (for Traditional FEHB & PSHB) or 1-855-344-0930 (for MPDP FEHB & PSHB). In addition, your provider can request the formulary criteria. Please contact your provider for details. My GLP-1 formulary exception criteria was approved, what do I do now? If your formulary exception is approved, here is the breakdown of what tier your drug will be in for 2026 based on which BCBS FEP plan you have: Drug Tier FEP Blue Focus® FEP Blue Basic® FEP Blue Standard® Tier 2: Preferred Wegovy Zepbound Tier 3: Non-Preferred Zepbound Zepbound For FEP Blue Basic and FEP Blue Standard, Zepbound (tirzepatide) would be Tier 3 non-preferred. For FEP Blue Focus, Wegovy (semaglutide) and Zepbound (tirzepatide) would be Tier 2 Preferred. Even if your GLP-1 gets approved, you cannot request a tier exception. Will my approved formulary exception still apply next year? Approved formulary exceptions will be recognized through their stated expiration date. For information on non-covered/excluded drugs, visit the FEHB Prescription Drug Coverage or PSHB Prescription Drug Coverage page. My GLP-1 formulary exception criteria was denied, what do I do now? Please discuss the other GLP-1 and weight loss medications on the formulary with your healthcare provider. If you and your provider decide that you must be on the non-covered/excluded weight loss GLP-1, please refer to the denial letter mailed to you and your provider that will explain your appeal rights and steps you may take. My GLP-1 formulary exception criteria expired, what do I do now? Please discuss the other GLP-1 and weight loss medications on the formulary with your healthcare provider. If you and your provider decide that you must be on the non-covered/excluded weight loss GLP-1, your provider must request a new formulary exception to continue accessing your medication.
```

## Researcher notes (interpretation, not verbatim)

- In the "DRUGS NOT COVERED IN 2026" tables (Standard p.124, Basic p.120) the not-covered drugs in that row are "ADIPEX-P, PLENITY, ZEPBOUND"; the remaining names (benzphetamine ... SAXENDA, WEGOVY, XENICAL (RX)) are the "COVERED OPTIONS" column. Column boundaries are inferred from extracted text order.
- A search of the full extracted text of the FEP Blue Focus formulary (Effective 08/07/2026) returned no occurrence of "Wegovy" or "Zepbound"; its ANTI-OBESITY AGENTS section lists only liraglutide (weight management) and orlistat. Wegovy therefore appears non-formulary on Focus (consistent with the Caremark PA form Q7 "Blue Focus Patient: ... This is a non-formulary medication" and the FAQ tier table placing Wegovy at Tier 2 on Focus only after an approved formulary exception).
